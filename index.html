Research Interests

Exploring the frontiers of virtual reality, gaming, and interactive technologies

Game AI & Behavior Trees (게임 인공지능)

Advanced game artificial intelligence using behavior trees, goal-oriented planning, and utility-based decision making for realistic and adaptive game agents.

Virtual & Augmented Reality

Pioneering research in VR, AR, and Mixed Reality technologies for immersive experiences and applications.

Interactive Authoring Tools

Development of authoring tools for Virtual Reality systems, including the P-VoT platform for structured VR development.

E-Learning & Edutainment

Creating engaging educational content through entertainment technologies and interactive learning experiences.
